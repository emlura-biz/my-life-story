# Plan: Letter to Future Me

A new page in the scrapbook for the child to write (or record) a letter to their future self.

## What we decided

- **Who writes it:** The child. If they're too young to write, they can record a voice message instead.
- **When it's read:** Whenever they want — always available, not locked or time-delayed.
- **Where it lives:** Its own separate page in the scrapbook.

## What the page contains

- A title
- A writing area
- Five prompts to help the child know what to include:
  1. What are you hoping for?
  2. What do you want your future self to know?
  3. What makes you feel happy?
  4. What are you proud of?
  5. Who makes you feel safe?

## Build order

1. Add the page with the title, writing area, and prompts (text version first)
2. Add the voice recording option for younger children

## What could go wrong

- The voice recording needs the child's browser to have microphone access — some devices or school settings block this
- The new page needs to match the existing look: dark/light theme, fonts, navigation arrows
- The writing needs to save automatically (like the rest of the book already does)
